Identify Skin Type

        Not sure about your skin type?
        Follow these steps to identify your skin better.

Steps to Identify Your Skin Type

  1. Wash your face with a gentle cleanser.
  2. Pat your face dry with a clean towel.
  3. Wait for your skin to return to its normal state before applying any products.
  4. Observe how your skin feels and looks.
