What is Oily Skin?

Oily skin produces more sebum (natural skin oil), which can make the skin look shiny.

Common Characteristics

  • Excess oil production
  • Shiny appearance
  • Skin may feel greasy
  • Pores may look more visible
  • May be prone to clogged pores

Basic Skincare Routine

  1. Wash gently with a mild cleanser.
  2. Use a lightweight moisturizer.
  3. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

Ingredients to Know

  • Niacinamide:

    May help manage excess oil, Supports skin barrier function and reduces inflammation.

  • Salicylic Acid:

    Helps unclog pores, reduce blackheads and whiteheads, Useful for acne-prone skin.

  • Hyaluronic Acid:

    Helps the skin retain moisture, Keeps skin hydrated for long time.

Precautions

  • Avoid Harsh Cleansers
  • Don't Overwash Face
  • Choose Lightweight Products
  • Never Skip Moisturizer
  • Apply Sunscreen Daily
  • Avoid Squeezing Pimples
Note: Everyone's skin is different. Introduce new products carefully and stop if irritation occurs.
