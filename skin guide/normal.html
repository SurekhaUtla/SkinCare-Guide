What is Normal Skin?

Normal skin generally feels comfortable and balanced, without excessive oiliness or dryness.

Common Characteristics

  • Generally balanced oil production
  • Skin feels comfortable
  • Usually no excessive dryness or oiliness
  • Small or less visible pores
  • Good Hydration
  • Balanced appearance

Basic Skincare Routine

  1. Cleanse gently with a mild cleanser.
  2. Apply a suitable moisturizer.
  3. Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen daily.
  4. (optional) Hydrating serum, if needed.

Ingredients to Know

  • Hyaluronic Acid:

    Helps the skin retain moisture and stay hydrated.

  • Niacinamide:

    May support the skin barrier and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.

  • Ceramides:

    Help support the skin's natural protective barrier.

  • Aloe Vera:

    Provides a soothing, refreshning feel.

Precautions

  • Avoid harsh cleansers.
  • Do not overwash your face.
  • Choose products suitable for your skin.
  • Keep your skin moisturized.
  • Apply sunscreen daily.
  • Introduce new products carefully.

Note: Everyone's skin is different. These characteristics are general guidelines, not a medical diagnosis.
