What is Dry Skin?

Dry skin may lack sufficient moisture and can feel tight, rough, or flaky. It may sometimes feel uncomfortable, especially after washing.

Common Characteristics

  • Skin may feel tight or rough.
  • Flaky or peeling patches may appear.
  • Skin may look dull.
  • Itching or irritation may occur.
  • Fine lines may appear more noticeable.

Basic Skincare Routine

  1. Use a gentle, non-stripping cleanser.
  2. Apply a moisturizer to help retain moisture.
  3. Choose a richer cream if your skin needs extra hydration.
  4. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen daily.

Ingredients to Know

  • Hyaluronic Acid:

    Helps the skin retain moisture.

  • Ceramides:

    Help support the skin's natural protective barrier.

  • Glycerin:

    Attracts water and helps keep the skin hydrated.

Precautions

  • Avoid harsh soaps and cleansers.
  • Avoid very hot water when washing your face.
  • Do not scrub flaky skin harshly.
  • Apply moisturizer regularly.
  • Use sunscreen daily.
  • Introduce new skincare products carefully.

Note: Everyone's skin is different. These characteristics are general guidelines, not a medical diagnosis. If dryness is severe or persistent, consider consulting a dermatologist.
