What is Combination Skin?

Combination skin has both oily and dry or normal areas. The forehead, nose, and chin (T-zone) may be oily, while the cheeks may feel dry or normal.

Common Characteristics

  • Forehead and nose may look shiny.
  • Cheeks may feel dry or normal.
  • Pores may be more visible in the T-zone.
  • Different areas of the face may need different care.
  • Oiliness may change with weather or skincare products.

Basic Skincare Routine

  1. Use a gentle cleanser for your whole face.
  2. Apply a lightweight moisturizer where needed.
  3. Use extra moisturizer on dry areas if necessary.
  4. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen daily.

Ingredients to Know

  • Niacinamide:

    May help manage the appearance of excess oil and support the skin barrier.

  • Hyaluronic Acid:

    Helps the skin retain moisture.

  • Ceramides:

    Help support the skin's natural protective barrier, especially in dry areas.

Precautions

  • Avoid harsh cleansers.
  • Do not overwash your face to remove oil.
  • Avoid using too many active ingredients at once.
  • Choose products suitable for your skin.
  • Do not skip moisturizer on oily areas.
  • Apply sunscreen daily.

Note: Everyone's skin is different. These characteristics are general guidelines, not a medical diagnosis. Introduce new products carefully and stop if irritation occurs.
